Description

Road Trip Roulette is a roguelite driving game about a broke musician with one night to cross Washington State for the gig that could change everything. The route is real — 293 miles from Seattle over Snoqualmie Pass, across the Columbia at Vantage, and through the Palouse to Pullman — and so are the problems: fuel, hunger, sleep, snow, crosswinds, an aging engine, and the Washington State Patrol. Ten music genres define ten playable identities, each with its own car, handling profile, economic quirks, and original soundtrack. Runs end in jail, in a ditch, or on stage. Then it's Friday again.

Features

  • Ten genres, ten machines — from a phonk VIP Sedan to a norteño Custom Pickup, each with distinct strengths, weaknesses, and top speed.
  • 100+ original songs — a full soundtrack per genre, playable on the website.
  • A real 293-mile route — 19 real towns with real facts, mountain snow to desert wind in one run.
  • Survival on wheels — fuel, food, drinks, bladder, alertness, engine temperature, and hit points, all arguing at once.
  • Missions & reputation — fares, cargo, and timed jobs with payout multipliers that scale to ×5.
  • Cops with a memory — warnings, tickets, wanted stars, and chases.
  • Plates as identity — globally unique license-plate usernames and a world leaderboard for score, miles, and fastest run.

History

Road Trip Roulette is a solo-developed game built in Washington State about driving across Washington State. It grew out of a long-running fascination with the I-90 corridor — a drive where the weather, the landscape, and the radio all change three times before you get there.
