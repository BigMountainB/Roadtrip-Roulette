# Road Trip Roulette Story Comic Art Manifest

This folder contains clean, text-free master artwork for the persistent story comic described in Chapter 18 of `Road Trip Roulette Overview.md`. Runtime dialogue, speech balloons, relationship flashes, mood icons, chosen vehicle overlays, and dynamic band names must be rendered by the game rather than baked into these images.

## Master specifications

- Conversation panel: 1672 x 941 PNG (16:9 landscape), crop-safe for current iPhones.
- Reference sheet: 1536 x 1024 PNG.
- Look: cinematic near-photographic painted realism matching the website comic.
- Good outcomes: warm red/gold grade. Bad outcomes: cold blue/green grade.
- Keep faces, outfits, vehicle damage, cargo damage, weather, and passenger placement continuous.
- Do not put readable text, speech balloons, captions, UI, relationship frames, or mood icons into panel artwork.
